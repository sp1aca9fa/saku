# Indicators and signals

Formulas, parameters and warm-up of every indicator (requirements 06), and how signals turn them into a state. Code: `packages/core/src/strategy/`. The reference implementation (`packages/reference`) computes the same independently; external golden values (testfol, TradingView) take precedence over both.

## Series

- **Price** (`totalReturn: false`): closes split-adjusted to the latest share basis (a later split rescales all past values by the same factor, so comparisons with the fund's own average and volatility do not change). A comparison with a fixed level does change (a close of 15 above a level of 10 reads 7.5 after a 2-for-1 split), so a plain `price` or `sma` against a `threshold` is refused at parse, by `pnpm strategy check` and registration (decisions log 2026-11-01, C18 (a)): compare the fund with its own average instead. A total-return series against a fixed level depends on where the stored history starts (the index is 1.0 on the first stored day), and comparing two funds' price levels changes with either one's split; whether to refuse those too is open (owner decision C26). `volatility` against a threshold is allowed (a percentage, not rescaled).
- **Total return** (`totalReturn: true`): our total-return index (`docs/data-pipeline.md`), dividends reinvested on the ex-date.

## Indicators (A2)

| Indicator | Formula | Parameters | Warm-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| `price` | The series value on the date | ticker, totalReturn | none |
| `sma` | Mean of the last `length` series values | length >= 1 | length - 1 bars |
| `volatility` | Sample standard deviation (n - 1) of the last `length` simple daily returns of the series, x sqrt(252), x 100 (percent per year) | length >= 2 | length bars |
| `threshold` | A fixed number (volatility thresholds in percent) | value | none |

Each value is computed from its own window, so a longer history never changes it (incremental and full recomputation agree).

Assumptions to settle with golden values: volatility uses the sample standard deviation (n - 1) and simple returns; testfol's or TradingView's definition may use n or log returns.

## Indicators (B1, v1)

The contract for both `packages/core` and `packages/reference`; each implements it from this text alone. `v` is the series (price or total return), `N` is `length`, `ln` the natural log. Percent values are x 100.

| Indicator | Formula | Parameters | Warm-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ema` | alpha = 2 / (N + 1). The first value (at bar N - 1) is the mean of the first N values; then EMA_t = alpha x v_t + (1 - alpha) x EMA_t-1 | length >= 1 | length - 1 bars |
| `return` | (v_t / v_t-N - 1) x 100 | length >= 1 | length bars |
| `volatility`, `method: close` | As in A2 above (the default when `method` is left out) | length >= 2 | length bars |
| `volatility`, `method: parkinson` | sqrt(252 x S / (4 ln 2 x N)) x 100, S = sum over the last N days of (ln(H/L))^2 | length >= 1 | length - 1 bars |
| `volatility`, `method: garman_klass` | sqrt(252 x mean over the last N days of [0.5 (ln(H/L))^2 - (2 ln 2 - 1)(ln(C/O))^2]) x 100 | length >= 1 | length - 1 bars |
| `volatility`, `method: yang_zhang` | sqrt(252 x (s_o + k s_c + (1 - k) s_rs)) x 100 over the last N days: s_o the sample variance (n - 1) of ln(O_t / C_t-1), s_c the sample variance of ln(C_t / O_t), s_rs the mean of ln(H/C) ln(H/O) + ln(L/C) ln(L/O), k = 0.34 / (1.34 + (N + 1) / (N - 1)) | length >= 2 | length bars |
| `drawdown` | (v_t / max(v over the last N values, today included) - 1) x 100, at most 0. Without `length`: the maximum since the series' first value (all-time high) | length >= 1 or none | length - 1 bars, or none |
| `high`, `low` | The maximum (minimum) of the last N series values, today included | length >= 1 | length - 1 bars |

- **Open, high and low on the series' basis:** each day's O, H and L are scaled by the same factor as that day's series value against its raw close (price: the split adjustment; total return: the index value / the raw close). Ratios within a day (H/L, C/O) are then the raw ones; the overnight ratio O_t / C_t-1 includes the day's dividend and split. A day without an open, high or low gives no value for any window holding it (never estimated from the close). All four of the day's values are scaled the same way: series O, H, L, C = raw O, H, L, C x (series value / raw close), so equal raw values stay exactly equal and a flat day gives exactly 0. A missing open, high or low, a high below a low, an open or close outside [low, high], or a non-positive or non-finite value is a data error (layer A reports an inconsistent bar; a missing open, high or low and a wrong but consistent one are not checked yet, so range methods are refused at registration until a layer B check of them exists, decision C32); every range method (`parkinson`, `garman_klass`, `yang_zhang`) gives no value for any window holding that day. For `yang_zhang` the day before the window supplies only its close. A negative or non-finite variance gives no value. `return` and `drawdown` give no value when a value they read is not positive or not finite (the all-time `drawdown` from that day on); a non-finite `ema` gives no value; `high` and `low` give no value when a value they read is not finite.
- **History dependence:** `ema` and the all-time `drawdown` depend on every value since the series' first one; incremental and full recomputation still agree (same stored history), but a history that starts earlier (a longer refetch) changes them slightly. Every other indicator depends only on its own window.
- **Fixed levels:** `ema`, `high` and `low` on plain prices against a `threshold` are refused like `price` and `sma` (C18 (a)); `return`, `volatility` and `drawdown` are percentages and allowed. A plain price-type value (`price`, `sma`, `ema`, `high`, `low`) against a percentage (`return`, `volatility`, `drawdown`) is refused too (a split halves the price, not the percentage). Total-return levels (against a level or a percentage) and cross-fund price levels: open (C26).

Assumed, to settle with golden values (TradingView, testfol): the EMA seed is the mean of the first N values (TradingView's convention); `high`/`low` read series values (closes on the series' basis), not the day's highs and lows; `return` is simple, not log.

## Signals

- **compare:** `left op right`, `op` is `>` or `<`. With `tolerancePct` t, for `>`: true when left > right + |right| x t/100, false when left < right - |right| x t/100, otherwise unchanged (`<` mirrors it). For a positive reference this is requirements 05's right x (1 +- t/100); the absolute value keeps the band symmetric and the upper bound above the lower one when the reference is negative (a drawdown, a negative return), as 05's "symmetric hysteresis" intends (decisions log 2026-11-01). A reference of exactly 0 gives a band of no width (see C27). The first valid day is a plain comparison.
- **Near-ties:** a value within 1e-9 (relative) of the bound it would cross is a tie: the state does not change, and the tie is logged. On the first day a tie with the reference is false.
- **ensemble:** members are compare signals, each with its own state; the output is the share of members true; the signal is true when the share is at least `threshold` (assumption: "at least", so 1 of 2 is true at 0.5).
- **not / and / or** (B1): `{ id, type: not, of: <id> }` is true when the named signal is false; `{ id, type: and | or, of: [<id>, <id>, ...] }` (at least 2 different ids) is true when all / at least one of them is true. They read the other signals' states on the same date (compare, ensemble or another derived signal) and keep no state of their own (no tolerance band, no near-tie: their inputs already applied theirs). Defined on a date when every signal they read is defined. An unknown id, a reference to itself or a cycle is refused at parse; the order in the file does not matter.
- **Allocations:** the first allocation whose `when` holds is active (`all`: every named signal true; `any`: at least one; `none`: none of them); the last has no `when`.
- A date is evaluated when every ticker the signals read has a bar that day, from the first date every signal is defined. On a date that is not evaluated because one operand has no value (for example a range volatility over a day without a low), the comparisons that do have values still update their tolerance-band state (core and reference agree; noted in the backlog for review).

## Equity curves (backtests)

- **Execution:** `next_open` (default): a decision at a close is traded at the next session's open; `next_close`: at the next close; `same_close`: at the deciding close, for testfol parity only and labeled "not achievable".
- **Returns:** close to close; with `next_open` the session is split at the open: the night (previous close to open) goes to the old holdings, with the ex-date dividend and the split; the day (open to close) to the new ones. A held ticker's return from one session to the next includes every bar of that ticker dated in between (a date that is not a session because another ticker the strategy may hold has no bar there): from its last bar on or before the previous session, the in-between bars' closes, splits and dividends are compounded in date order into the next session's night part (or its whole day), never dropped. Assumed (B1, 2026-11-02): a synthetic ticker takes L x that compounded base return (no daily reset inside the gap), and financing, expense and cash accrue once per session, as without a gap.
- **Cash** (`CASH_USD`): the USD rate (percent a year, as known before the session) / 252 per night held; none for the open-to-close part.
- **Synthetic leverage** (`SPY?L=3&E=0.9`): L x the base's return, minus (L - 1) x the cash rate / 252 and E / 100 / 252 per night held. One grammar for every ticker (strategy schema, backtest, metrics; `packages/core/src/strategy/ticker.ts`): `BASE` or `BASE?k=v[&k=v]`, where k is `L` (leverage, 1 without it) or `E` (expense, percent a year, 0 without it), each at most once and in either order, and v a plain decimal (`-?digits[.digits]`: no exponent, `+`, spaces, hex or URL escapes); L is finite and > 0, E is finite and >= 0; BASE is non-empty, without whitespace or `? & = %`; `CASH_USD` takes no parameters. Anything else (`X?L=`, `X?L`, `X?L=1e1`, `X?L=0x3`, `X?L=%33`, `X?L= 3`, `X?L=2&L=3`, `X?l=2`, `CASH_USD?L=2`) is refused: the schema refuses the strategy at parse with the ticker's path and the reason, and reading one elsewhere throws (never a silent 0 or NaN). Assumed: L > 0 (a short or 0x synthetic is not in the requirements). Parameters apply to holdings only: an indicator operand reads its ticker's own bars by the plain name, so the schema refuses an operand ticker with parameters (`SPY?L=3`) at parse, with its path.
- **Days without a decision** (B1): a date that is not evaluated (an operand has no value, e.g. a range volatility over a day without a low) still counts in the equity curve: the holdings' returns of every session are compounded, never skipped. On such a date the backtest does what the forward record does: the strategy's safe state, traded like any decision (next session, costs). A decision placed before the gap is traded at the first session after its close, as usual. A session is a date on which every ticker the strategy may hold (any allocation's holdings and the safe state's, cash and synthetic tickers through their base ticker or rate) has a bar, from the first evaluated date on; a date where a ticker the signals read has no bar is then a session without a decision (safe state). Callers load bars for every ticker the strategy may hold, the safe state included. A strategy that may hold only cash has no ticker to take sessions from: its sessions are the evaluated dates.
- **Weights:** set to the allocation's weights at each trade, then drift with prices until the allocation changes (no rebalancing within an allocation; assumption until testfol parity says otherwise).
- **Costs:** (slippage bps / 10,000 + fee %) x turnover at each trade, turnover being the sum of absolute weight changes (an entry from nothing counts 1, a full switch 2).
- **Summary:** CAGR over calendar time (365.25-day years), maximum drawdown from the previous peak.
- **Whole shares** (B1; requirements 05 "Execution model", small amounts): an optional run setting, `wholeShares: { initialAmount: '<decimal text>', currency: 'USD' }`, not a strategy field (a registered strategy is unchanged). Without it, results are exactly the fractional ones above. The rest of the model (sessions, execution times, when a trade happens, safe state) is unchanged; only the bookkeeping differs:
  - *Input:* the amount is plain decimal text (digits, optionally a point and digits; no sign, exponent, grouping or spaces) above 0. Only `USD` for now; another currency is refused with the reason (base-currency cash and FX come later). A target listing a ticker twice (any allocation's, the safe state's or a day's holdings) is refused before anything runs. A cost rate (slippage bps / 10,000 + fee % / 100) of 100% or more is refused (the budget below would not stay above 0). A strategy that may hold a synthetic ticker (one with parameters, `X?L=3`; any allocation's, the safe state's or a day's holdings) is refused with the reason: it has no traded price, and one built from its returns would depend on the backtest's start date, so its share counts would mean nothing.
  - *Prices:* a holding trades at its own price at the execution time (the open with `next_open`, the close otherwise), as traded: every ticker it may hold must have bars that are not split-adjusted, or the run is refused (a share count on today's split basis is not a share count anyone held). A missing open at a `next_open` trade, or a price at or below 0 at a trade, is refused.
  - *Before the first trade* the initial amount is not invested and earns nothing (the fractional curve stays at 1 there too).
  - *At each trade:* with p each ticker's price, `V` = residual cash + the sum of shares x p over the holdings, `H` = the same sum without the cash, c = slippage bps / 10,000 + fee % / 100. The budget is `B = V - c x H` (the cost of selling everything held is set aside first). Each non-cash holding of the target (weight in percent) gets n shares: `x = (weight x B) / (100 x p x (1 + c))`, computed in that order; if x is within 1e-12 (relative, |x - k| <= 1e-12 x |x|) of a whole number k, x is k (this snap only absorbs float noise, a few ulps of about 1e-16: 58 x 100 / 200 is 29, which `0.58 x 100 / 2` misses; a true quotient like 6.999999993 stays 6); then `n = floor(x)` (never rounded up: the account cannot spend money it lacks). Costs are c x the sum of |n new - n old| x p over every ticker held before or after (only the shares actually traded). Residual cash = `V - sum(n x p) - costs`, held as `CASH_USD` (the target's own `CASH_USD` weight included). This keeps the residual at 0 or above (`sum(n x p) x (1 + c) <= B` and costs <= c x (sum(n x p) + H)), up to the snap, which can overspend by at most 1e-12 x B (B <= V). A residual below 0 by at most 1e-9 x V is float noise and is 0; more fails. The two tolerances are a factor 1000 apart, so the snap can never trip the residual check (the split snap below keeps 1e-9: it spends nothing). A target ticker that gets 0 shares is listed with the trade; it is not an error.
  - *Between trades* share counts are fixed and the value moves with prices. In each night part (or whole day), in this order: the residual cash earns the USD cash rate / 252, like `CASH_USD`; the rate is read only when the cash at the start of the part is above 0 (before that part's dividend or fraction cash arrives), and an unknown rate then fails. Then each of the ticker's bars of the part, in date order: the bars of a gap (see "Returns") first, then the session's own bar. On each, shares are multiplied by the split factor; a product within 1e-9 (relative) of a whole number is that number; otherwise the whole shares are kept and the fraction is paid as cash at that bar's close (a gap bar) or at the part's end price (the session's bar: the open for the night, the close for the whole day). Then the dividend, on the whole shares kept after that bar's split (shares kept x the bar's dividend per share), is added to the cash. A reverse split can leave 0 shares: the holding is then all cash.
  - *Result:* each point adds `value`, the amount at the close with its currency (`{ amount, currency: 'USD' }`); `equity` is value / initial amount (1 until the first trade); `weights` are each holding's share of the value, `CASH_USD` the residual's (when above 0). Each trade records its date, the execution time (open or close), V, the share counts of the target's non-cash tickers, those with 0 shares, the residual cash and the costs; money amounts carry their currency. `costs` sums cost / V per trade; `switches` counts as above. Amounts are float64 estimates.
  - Assumed (the spec sized on V, which lets the residual go below 0 on a switch with costs: selling X and buying Y for V / (1 + c) leaves -c x V; the conservative reading sets the sale cost aside first): the budget `B`; dividends are paid to cash, not reinvested, until the next trade; cash in lieu of a split's fraction at that part's end price; a split-adjusted source is refused rather than un-split (the un-split needs every split up to the source's basis date, which the bars may not reach).
